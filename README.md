# Coca-Cola-sales-Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing Coca-Cola's beverage brand sales performance across the United States for the year 2022.

1.Visualizations included:

KPI Cards (Total Units Sold & Operating Profit)
Brand-wise Sales Summary Table
Key Influencers AI Visual (Operating Profit Analysis)
Geographic Bubble Map (Sales by US State)


2. Process Overview 📋
The raw sales data was cleaned, structured, and loaded into Power BI using Power Query. DAX measures were created to calculate key business metrics such as total sales, operating profit, and operating margin. The report layout was designed with a consistent color theme, cross-filtering enabled across all visuals, and a date slicer for dynamic filtering.

3.Tech Stack 🛠️ 
ToolUsage Power BI Desktop,Report building & visualizationPower Query,Data cleaning & transformation,DAX Calculated measures 

4.Data Source 📂

Source: Coca-Cola sales dataset sourced from a public dataset website
Period Covered: 2022 (March 2022 – December 2022)
Data includes: Beverage brand names, invoice dates, units sold, total sales, average price, operating profit, and operating margin


5.Features & Highlights ✅

13.18 Million+ total units sold across all beverage brands in 2022
$25.86 Lakh total operating profit identified across all brands
Coca-Cola emerged as the top-performing brand in both sales and profit
Fanta, Sprite, and Powerade were identified as the key influencers causing a decrease in operating profit
Geographic map reveals state-level sales distribution across the United States
Date slicer allows dynamic filtering of all visuals by invoice date
